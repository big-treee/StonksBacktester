# Quantitative Research Platform

The StonksBacktester goes beyond single simulations acting as a robust Research Platform designed to optimize validate and stress test quantitative models.

## Optimization Engines

1. **Grid Search**: Evaluates every parameter combination systematically across a defined search space.
2. **Random Search**: Samples parameter combinations randomly to discover optimal parameters efficiently.
3. **Walk Forward Optimization**: Evaluates out of sample performance across rolling in sample and out of sample windows.
4. **Monte Carlo Simulation**: Shuffles trade trade returns and parameters to evaluate strategy robustness under simulated market paths.
